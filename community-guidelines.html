Community Guidelines

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Share stories, reflections, and poetry in the language you enjoy. Help make this a welcoming place.

Respect others

Discuss ideas without attacking their authors. No threats, harassment, hateful attacks, exploitation, or encouragement of violence.

Protect privacy

Do not post another person's address, phone number, identifying documents, private messages, or sensitive information without permission. Take particular care with children's images and identifying information.

Share honestly

Use your own writing and images or obtain permission. Credit sources and quotations. Distinguish opinion from factual claims. No impersonation, scams, repetitive promotion, unlawful material, explicit sexual content, graphic violence, or fake engagement.

Report problems

Signed-in readers can use Report on a journal or comment. Choose a reason and explain the concern. Reports are private and reviewed by the administrator; reporting does not automatically hide content. Alternatively, email mdhasmat7@gmail.com with the journal URL and concern. Never include passwords or unnecessary sensitive documents.
